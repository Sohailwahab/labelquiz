LabelQuiz

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

LabelQuiz turns labeled diagrams into self-testing activities. We designed it to keep your study materials private and under your control.

Local processing

Images and OCR text are processed in your browser whenever practical. Saved quizzes are stored on your device unless you choose to connect Google Drive.

Optional Google Drive connection

If you choose “Connect Google,” LabelQuiz requests the limited Google Drive app-data permission. This lets LabelQuiz create, read, update, and delete only its own hidden application data. LabelQuiz cannot browse or read your ordinary Google Drive files.

How data is used

Your images, labels, and quiz data are used only to provide LabelQuiz features, including saving and restoring your quizzes. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Your choices

Connecting Google is optional. You can disconnect at any time. You can also revoke LabelQuiz access from your Google Account permissions. Local browser data can be removed using your browser’s site-data controls.

Google API Services

LabelQuiz’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to labelquiz-support@googlegroups.com.
